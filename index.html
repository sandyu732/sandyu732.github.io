A thought

Opportunity for all

Talent and hard work get most of the credit for how careers turn out — but they're only two pieces of the story. The third, quieter piece is opportunity: the doors that happen to be open to you, the person who happens to take a chance on you, the room you happen to get into. Two people can bring the same effort and talent to their work and still walk very different paths, simply because one of them ran into a hidden barrier the other never had to see.

A single opportunity can bridge the gap between hard work and success.

That's not a reason to discount hard work — it's a reason to take seriously the part of the equation we can actually influence for other people: building fairer systems, and being the person who opens a door for someone else the way one was once opened for you. Real success isn't just the finish line you cross — it's counted, too, in the opportunities you help create along the way.
